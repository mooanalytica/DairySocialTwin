# Data Access and Redistribution

The underlying data were collected at a commercial dairy farm and are subject
to restricted-access agreements. They are not included in this repository and
must not be uploaded to GitHub, published as supplementary material, or
redistributed to unauthorized people or organizations.

Restricted material includes, but is not limited to:

- raw, tracked, cached, corrected, or otherwise processed video containing
  farm imagery;
- human review annotations and identity assignments;
- detection, tracking, keypoint, and corrected-result CSV files;
- model weights, full private configuration files, manifests, and processed
  results derived from the farm data.

Authorized project members may retrieve required assets from the internal
Dropbox area under:

```text
Yiwen Huang SNA Project Summer 2026/private_assets/re-identification-ANNO/
```

Files must be restored to their documented repository-relative locations.

The source code contains fixed internal paths, clip identifiers, hashes, and
aggregate validation constants, but no farm-video RGB data. Access to the
restricted assets must be arranged through the project data custodian or
principal investigator. Possession of this source repository does not grant
permission to access or redistribute the underlying data.
